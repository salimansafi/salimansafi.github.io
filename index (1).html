About me

A final-year student who likes making networks work and making repetitive tasks disappear.

I’m in my final year at Nangarhar University, studying in the network and IT department. My work sits between two areas: building and troubleshooting networks, and writing software that automates them.

On the networking side I work with routing, switching, IP addressing and subnetting, VLANs, and network security basics, and I practice in simulators such as Cisco Packet Tracer and GNS3. I’m following the Cisco certification path from CCNA toward CCNP and CCIE.

On the programming side I use Python and JavaScript. I’m interested in network automation: scripts that log in to devices, back up configurations, check network health and apply changes consistently with tools like Netmiko.
